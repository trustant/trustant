Ubuntu / Debian per-package licensing

The Ubuntu 24.04 base system is a collection of independently licensed
packages, mostly under the GPL, LGPL, BSD, MIT and similar free software
licenses. Every installed package ships its full copyright and license
statement as a machine-readable Debian copyright file at

    /usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright

inside the container image (or the WSL2/Lima Ubuntu guest). Those files are
the authoritative license texts for the packages. Corresponding sources are
available from the Ubuntu archive with `apt-get source <package>` or at
https://launchpad.net/ubuntu.
